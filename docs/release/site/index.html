UltraEdge support

UltraEdge is a free race preparation app for ultramarathon runners and crews. Build an event plan, organize checkpoints, gear and drop bags, assign crew roles, import a GPX route, and share a PDF race plan.

Where are my plans saved?

Editable plans are saved on this device. Optional team rooms share race timing and logistics across phones. Full editable plans do not have automatic cloud backups or device transfer. A shared PDF is a readable copy; it cannot restore your plan into the app.

Before travel, export a PDF and save it somewhere you can access offline. Check that the PDF contains the information your crew needs. Keep your original GPX file separately.

What works offline?

Previously saved plans and locally imported GPX routes can be read without an internet connection. Map backgrounds may need a connection. Check your route and PDF in airplane mode before race day. UltraEdge is a planning tool and does not provide live tracking, crew notifications, or turn-by-turn navigation.

A change did not save

Keep the app installed. Read the error message, return to the list, and refresh before trying again. Check that your device has available storage. If a problem persists, record the steps that led to it and report it below. Deleting the app to troubleshoot can remove your plans.

Remove my data

You can delete individual events, crew members, gear and drop bags in the app. To remove the app’s local data from your iPhone, choose Delete App in iOS, rather than Offload App. There is currently no single in-app action to clear all data. Copies you exported or shared, and any device backups, must be managed separately.

Report a problem or suggest an improvement

Use the UltraEdge GitHub issue tracker. Include your app version, iPhone/iPad model, iOS version, what you expected, and what happened. GitHub may require an account to post.

Issues are public. Do not attach private routes, crew phone numbers, personal health information, or screenshots containing them. For a sensitive concern, first request a private contact method without including the sensitive details.
