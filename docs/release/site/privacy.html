UltraEdge privacy policy

Updated September 6, 2026. This policy describes UltraEdge 1.0’s free local planner.

Information stored on your device

UltraEdge stores the information you enter on your device: event plans and locations, checkpoints, gear, drop bags, crew names and contact details, role assignments, notes, profile information, weight entries, preferences, and imported GPX route files. These records support planning, display and PDF export.

Local planning records are not uploaded automatically. There are no subscriptions, advertising, or app-operated analytics and crash reporting enabled.

Optional live team races

Creating or joining a team room connects to Supabase in Canada and creates a guest identity, with a session saved securely on your device. Shared race timing, checkpoint names, stops, crew display names, vehicle/cargo labels and checkpoint observations are stored on the server and cached on joined phones. Crew contact details, personal notes and route files are excluded. Authentication also processes connection metadata such as IP addresses for abuse prevention.

Only joined members can retrieve the plan or submit observations. The owner can revoke membership or stop sharing and delete the room. Use “Delete my collaboration data” in the live team panel to remove your guest identity, owned rooms, memberships and reports. Deleting the app or a local race does not delete the server room. Previously saved copies held by others remain with them. Guest access has no email/password recovery; new phones need a new crew invitation. Pending reports remain on the device until synced.

Maps, files and other services

Displaying a map background uses the device’s map service and may make network requests for the area being viewed. This service operates under its provider’s privacy practices. On iOS, see Apple’s privacy information. UltraEdge does not provide live location tracking.

When you select a GPX file or an image, the system file or photo picker supplies the item you choose. A file stored with a cloud provider may need to download through that provider. Opening a race website or another external link uses the destination service’s privacy practices.

Sharing a race plan

PDF exports may include crew contact information, route details and notes from your plan. You choose whether and where to share them using the system share sheet. Review the PDF before sharing it. The app or person receiving a copy controls that copy; deleting the original plan does not remove shared copies.

Retention and deletion

Saved information remains locally until you remove it or delete the app. You can delete individual planning records in the app. There is currently no single in-app action to erase all local information. On iOS, use Delete App, rather than Offload App, to remove the app and its local data.

Exported files and copies held by other people or services must be deleted separately. Your operating system may include app data in device backups according to your settings; manage those backups through the relevant device or backup service. UltraEdge does not provide an editable-plan backup or restore service, and a PDF cannot restore an editable plan.

Support and privacy questions

The UltraEdge GitHub issue tracker is the development support contact. Information you post there is public and is handled under GitHub’s privacy statement. Do not post private routes, contact details or health information. For a sensitive concern, request a private contact method before sharing details.

Changes

This page will be updated when the app’s data practices change. Its date and version identify the practices it describes.
